## User corrections that still apply

- Main features/order: Goals, Tasks, Calendar. Board is a view of Goals, never a separate destination.
- No Planner, Plans, Focus, Library or other invented destinations. Habits are deferred. Analytics is liked for future work, not permission to invent its scope. Journal/log wording can describe progress records.
- Completion may offer an OPTIONAL explanation of how the task was done; must not force a second workflow or allow adding another task inside it. Preserve editing.
- Keep personal space private. Local shared-space UI must not pretend online accounts or sync exist. Owner creates/assigns work; manager is optional.
- Keep existing reminders, data import/export, custom colors, movement, planning and calendar operations accessible.
- Thin coherent icons; charcoal layers, coral primary accent, muted green success; no purple/yellow/gray theme. Deliberate smooth animations; honor reduced motion.
- The purpose is making life easier: what to do, what happened, and useful records. Manual planning must be excellent without AI. AI remains Work in progress.
